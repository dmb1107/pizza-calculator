## 1. What changed in the app

- **Mix sizes** print as a whole number or with ½, ⅓ or ⅔, in Panel 3's label,
  the badges and the log. Every key the envelope produces (3–24 balls ×
  240–300 g) prints exactly; a test sweeps all of them. 13 balls reads
  "6½-ball mix" and 19 balls "6⅓".
- **"Entered" means typed for this bake.** Each reading keeps the time it was
  last typed, and it counts when that is after the last reset of the steps. A
  value carried over in storage was typed before the reset. One arriving in a
  link was never typed on this device at all. Either way it stays a default
  until retyped. Typing the value already shown still counts.
- **Only the water can be confirmed.** The card says "Type it in Today's
  temperatures" beside room, flour and biga, and asks for a measurement beside
  a bowl prefill. It points the final temperature back to the end of its mix.
- **Phase times** sit beside their printed ranges on the card, flagged when
  outside. The card says a phase run to its cue can fall outside its range and
  the log corrects for that. If a timer ran on after its phase ended, the fix
  is to leave the mix out.
- **A bake is dated** by the day mix 1's Phase A timer started. Before that
  timer has started, the draft is dated by the day it would be saved.
- **Saving over the session's bake from an earlier date asks first.** The card
  replaces its Save button with the question, so nothing appears above a tap.
  It offers "Save as a new bake" first and "Replace the bake from {date}"
  second.

## 2. Three notes

1. **What "Save as a new bake" counts.** The case is a forgotten reset, so the
   new bake counts only readings typed after the earlier bake was last saved.
   Anything older belongs to that bake. The card shows the draft that way
   while it asks, so what the baker sees is what "Save as a new bake" writes.
   "Replace" keeps the session as it was. If you want this in §10, a sentence
   would do: *"Saved as a new bake, a session counts only readings typed after
   its earlier bake was saved."*
2. **Flour.** §10's list reads "Room, biga, bowl and final dough temperature
   count only when typed." Flour follows the same rule here: typed, or
   following the room. It may be worth naming in that sentence.
3. **The question appears before Phase A starts, too.** Until then the draft
   is dated by the day it would be saved, so a session carried over from an
   earlier bake asks from the moment its card is on screen. That matches "asks
   first", and it is the earliest point the baker can learn the reset was
   missed.
